STEAM classroom ideas ​

EcoCONNECT supports Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics through short, tangible activities.

Sample lesson hooks ​

SubjectPrompt
ScienceHow does light in the room change during the day?
TechnologyWhat does a sensor “tell” a computer?
EngineeringHow would you protect a board used outdoors?
ArtsTurn sensor values into color, sound, or a paper data poster
MathGraph 10 readings. What is the average? The range?

Facilitation tips ​

  • Keep groups small (2–3 students)
  • Aim for one clear question per session
  • End with a share-out: each group says one discovery
