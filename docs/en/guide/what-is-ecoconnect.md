# What is EcoCONNECT?

EcoCONNECT is an **open-source hardware** project for learning about the environment through simple sensors and hands-on making. Published for the community by hollyRock.

## Who is it for?

- Students and teachers exploring STEAM
- Clubs and makerspaces trying a first electronics project
- Anyone who wants to measure something real (light, temperature, and more) without a steep learning curve

## Open hardware, explained simply

“Open” means the designs are meant to be **studied, built, and improved together**.  
You do not need to read circuit files or firmware source to enjoy EcoCONNECT — start with the guides above. When you are ready to go deeper, visit [For makers](/en/makers/).
