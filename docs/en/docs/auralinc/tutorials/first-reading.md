# Your first EcoCONNECT

**Goal:** Watch a live value from the EcoCONNECT board and understand what it means.

**Time:** about 10 minutes

## Before you start

Finish [Getting started](/en/docs/hack/getting-started) and flash the firmware.

## Steps

1. Place the board on a clean, dry table.
2. Connect USB power.
3. Open the tool or serial monitor that your kit instructions recommend for beginners.
4. Watch the numbers for about 30 seconds without touching the sensor.
5. Gently change the environment (for example, cover a light sensor, or warm a temperature sensor with your hands — only if your kit says it is safe).
6. Watch how the numbers change.

## What to write in your notebook

- What stayed the same?
- What changed?
- Why do you think it changed?

## You’re done when…

You can explain one measurement in one sentence, for example:

> “When I covered the sensor, the light reading went down.”
