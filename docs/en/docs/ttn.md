# Connect to The Things Network

[The Things Network (TTN)](https://www.thethingsnetwork.org/) is a community that offers a free and open LoRaWAN network.
Volunteers around the world register gateways, and any community member (registration is free) can connect to a nearby gateway.
Of course, you can also register your own gateway with the community and contribute.

This page shows how to view the data from your own hacked EcoCONNECT through TTN.

## Steps to connect

- Prepare a hacked EcoCONNECT
- Look for a nearby gateway
- (If you find a gateway) Register as a TTN member and register the `deveui` of your EcoCONNECT
- Set up data reception
- Receive the data
